## Newsletter Title

gpt-oss-safeguard Makes Safety Policies Runtime Inputs

## Intro

OpenAI's gpt-oss-safeguard release turns written safety policies into runtime classifier inputs, which is useful for teams that need adaptable Trust and Safety workflows.

## Takeaways

- OpenAI published two open-weight safety reasoning models: gpt-oss-safeguard-120b and gpt-oss-safeguard-20b.
- Traditional classifiers are usually trained around a fixed taxonomy.
- The output is a classification under the supplied policy, with reasoning that developers and safety practitioners can inspect.
- The open question is whether policy-as-prompt classifiers become a common operations pattern.
