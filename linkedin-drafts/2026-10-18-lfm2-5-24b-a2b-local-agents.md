## Intro

Liquid AI's open-weight LFM2.5-24B-A2B activates 2B of its 24B parameters per token, supports tool use, and ships in formats for local agent runtimes.

## Takeaways

- LFM2.5-24B-A2B is the largest and final model in Liquid AI's LFM2.5 family, combining a reasoning-first checkpoint with broad support across local inference runtimes.
- Local agents need more than a model that writes plausible text.
- The first question is whether the benchmark strength survives real harness integrations across quantizations.
