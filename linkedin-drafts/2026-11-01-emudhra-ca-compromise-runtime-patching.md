## Newsletter Title

The eMudhra CA Compromise Is a Runtime Patch Problem

## Intro

Eight eMudhra intermediate CA keys were recoverable from public certificates. Browsers moved quickly, but runtimes, containers, proxies, and embedded clients need explicit trust-store updates and tests.

## Takeaways

- Mozilla's incident record says eight P-384 intermediate keys generated by eMudhra used private integers smaller than 256.
