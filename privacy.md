Effective August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to A2Techify Blogs at `blogs.a2techify.com`.

## Information We Collect

A2Techify Blogs is a static publication site. It does not include a contact form, account system, comment system, or checkout flow.

When you visit the blog, hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical information needed to serve and protect the site, such as IP address, browser information, request timestamps, requested URLs, and abuse-risk signals.

If you email us from a link on the blog, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, message content, and any follow-up communications.

## How We Use Information

We use information only as needed to:

- serve and protect the blog;
- respond to messages you send us;
- maintain business records and follow-up communications;
- detect spam, fraud, automated abuse, and security incidents;
- comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

## Service Providers

The blog is published with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information as part of hosting, security, logging, and abuse prevention. If you follow links to GitHub, LinkedIn, RSS readers, sample repositories, or other third-party sites, those services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information.

## Cookies And Analytics

The blog does not currently set analytics cookies or run Google Analytics. RSS readers, browsers, GitHub Pages, and linked third-party services may process technical information according to their own policies.

If analytics is added later, this page should be updated to describe what is collected and how visitors can control it.

## Retention And Security

We keep direct email communications only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted directly to us, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. You may also ask us to stop non-essential follow-up communication. Privacy rights vary by location, and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

## Children

This business-to-business blog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Main Site Policy

The main A2 Techify website has its own Privacy Policy at [www.a2techify.com/privacy](https://www.a2techify.com/privacy).
