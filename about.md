A2Techify Field Notes is where our engineering team records what we learn while evaluating new tools, models, and operating patterns.

We are usually trying to answer a few practical questions:

- What is the technology?
- Why is it happening now?
- What can a team do with it?
- What are the cost, security, and operational trade-offs?
- What is the smallest useful way to test it?

These are working notes, not launch-day summaries. We prefer primary sources, small reproducible examples, and an honest account of the limits. When a short command explains the idea, we use that. When a runnable repository is genuinely useful, we build one.

The goal is to leave another engineer with enough context to make a sound decision without repeating all of the discovery work.
